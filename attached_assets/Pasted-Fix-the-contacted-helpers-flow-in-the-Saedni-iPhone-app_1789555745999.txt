Fix the contacted-helpers flow in the Saedni iPhone app.

Current production defects:

1. When helpers contact a customer using Call or WhatsApp, they do not appear in the customer’s request details under contacted helpers.
2. When the customer completes the request, the helper selection list displays only the helper’s name without their phone number.

Required behavior:

A. Recording helper contact

- In the helper’s available-request screen, both buttons:
  - اتصال
  - واتساب
  must first call:
  POST /api/requests/:requestId/contact
- Send the correct contact method: phone or whatsapp.
- Record the contact successfully before opening the Phone or WhatsApp app.
- Derive helper_id from the authenticated user, not from a client-provided helper ID.
- Derive customer_id and contact_phone from the request/database.
- Do not trust client-provided phone numbers.
- Make repeated contact idempotent: the same helper must appear only once per request.
- Update last_contacted_at and the latest contact method on repeated contact.
- If contact recording fails, show a clear error and do not silently pretend the contact was recorded.

B. Customer request details

In the customer’s request details/history screen, add or fix the section:

المساعدون المتواصلون

For every helper who contacted regarding that request, display:

- Helper name
- Helper phone number
- Contact method: اتصال or واتساب
- Average star rating and rating count
- “جديد” when the helper has no ratings
- A direct call button
- A WhatsApp button

Load this list from:

GET /api/requests/:requestId/contacted-helpers

Only the request owner and authorized admin may access this information.

C. Request completion

When the customer marks a request as completed:

- Load only helpers recorded in request_contacts for that request.
- Display each option as:

  [Helper name]
  [Phone number]
  [Average rating · rating count]

- Do not show the name alone.
- The customer selects the helper who completed the work and submits a 1–5 star rating.
- Allow only one rating per completed request.
- If nobody contacted the customer, allow:
  لم أتلقَ مساعدة
  without selecting or rating a helper.

D. Backend verification

Inspect and verify:

- POST /api/requests/:id/contact
- GET /api/requests/:id/contacted-helpers
- PATCH /api/requests/:id/complete
- request_contacts table and its production migration
- Phone number authorization/redaction rules

Security rules:

- A helper cannot record contact on behalf of another helper.
- A helper cannot record contact for a request outside their authorized service areas.
- A customer cannot view contacted helpers for another customer’s request.
- Only helpers who actually recorded contact may appear in completion selection.
- Prevent duplicate contact rows.
- Do not expose phone numbers through general public user endpoints.

E. Historical limitation

Do not fabricate contacted-helper records for old requests. Contacts made before contact tracking was deployed cannot be reconstructed reliably. Show the contacted-helper list only from verified request_contacts records.

F. Required testing

Test the complete flow using the actual iPhone app:

1. Create a test request.
2. Sign in as a helper authorized for its area.
3. Press Call.
4. Confirm one request_contacts row is recorded.
5. Press WhatsApp for the same request.
6. Confirm the helper still has one contact summary row.
7. Sign in as the request owner.
8. Open the request details.
9. Confirm the helper’s name, phone number, method, and rating appear.
10. Complete the request.
11. Confirm the selection list displays both name and phone number.
12. Select and rate the helper.
13. Confirm duplicate rating is rejected.

Run API tests and mobile typecheck. Verify the actual authenticated iPhone screen, not a mockup.

Do not modify unrelated functionality or historical data.
